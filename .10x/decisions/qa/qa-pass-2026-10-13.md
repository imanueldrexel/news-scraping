## Lessons
- A closed word list for a language feature needs a held-out list before it closes. The dev's 22 adverbs passed the ticket's 5 and missed 6 of 6 common held-out ones.
- When one ticket's fix (SYS-145) depends on another's precondition (ADR-014 decision 3 on SYS-144), test the composition on the *served* path, not each on its own. Each passed its Done-when; together they regress real topic switches.
- An LLM step's refusal check is cheap to probe live. Odd inputs show what the model actually returns: it echoes and does not refuse.
- A pickle that grows after a same-length metadata rewrite is memo-sharing, not damage. Prove it by a per-document diff against the `.bak`, not by file size.
