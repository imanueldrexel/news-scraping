## Lessons
- A ranking rule tuned on the dev's question set needs held-out questions. The week window had 0 regressions on the dev's 15 and 3 on QA's 10.
- The floor is a tail filter: "prefer X when X passes the floor" turns it into a lead filter.
- For an LLM step with a deterministic fallback, measure the fallback on the same pairs. It is served whenever the key or balance fails, which was most of 2026-10-08..10-12.
- A real-process test (the worker kills, the child flushes) checks the contract that unit tests with fake clocks split into pieces.
