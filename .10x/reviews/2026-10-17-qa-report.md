## Lessons
- A test named for a Done-when item can pass without the behaviour it names. The sehingga test refuses on words, not on direction. **Mutate the behaviour itself**: here, swap the two halves of a correct sentence.
- A "one sentence" rule has to be measured in the source, not in the model's copy. Normalisation that strips punctuation also strips the sentence ends the rule depends on.
