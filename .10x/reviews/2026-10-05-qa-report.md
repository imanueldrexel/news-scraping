## Findings (QA filed)
1. **SYS-86 #246 (P2), echo guard hides distinct events.**
   - Over the 2,016 top × story pairs of the three v5 digests, `echoes()` flags 27. By hand, at least 11 are different events, e.g. "Presiden Prabowo berhentikan Wagub Babel" hidden by "Prabowo perintahkan audit haji", and "rumah subsidi" hidden by "BSU".
   - Cause: any capitalised non-initial word is a "name". That includes titles, place halves, programme names, months and `Rp2` tokens.
   - It was validated only on its own tuning days. It puts the CTO criterion "≤ 1 wrong hide" at risk, and can inflate the coverage metric.
2. **SYS-87 #247 (P3), the number guard ignores the decimal position.** `Rp1,5 triliun` passes against `Rp15 triliun`. Outlet-name digits (Liputan6) are in the corpus.
3. **OPS-44 #248 (P3), PDF upload failure.** The reader's message already said "Buka PDF untuk N berita utama". An upload failure leaves only an operator line with a local path.
4. **SYS-88 #249 (P3), HTML entities in headlines.** 311 of 43,829 headlines since 09-20 (JPNN 120, DETIK 60 incl. `&amp;amp;`, OKEZONE 58, …). The reader sees `&amp;` in the archive's link labels.
5. **DBT-56 #251, DBT-57 #252, DBT-58 #253 (P3), test gaps found by mutants** (see Mutation run).
6. **SYS-89 #250 (enhancement), cross-day repetition.** The edition repeats yesterday's: BSU is a top story with the same headline on 10-03 and 10-04, and ~9 of 32 items on 10-04 were in 10-03's edition.
