## Defects filed

- **#450 / #451 (major).** `_opening_subject` refuses every word in `common_words.txt`. That class is "lower case mid-sentence", so it holds nouns (gula, beras, nikel, timah, mandiri, emas, saham) as well as the time words and particles #448 needed. "Gula harganya naik?" or "Timah labanya berapa?" after a gold question is searched with Antam again. The fallback is the live path while the SumoPod key is at its budget.
- **#452 (minor, fail-open).** `movement_words.txt` admits 9 -nya nouns (eskalasinya, ekspornya, nilainya ...) and quantifiers (mayoritas, total, payung, kisaran, rata-rata). The module itself rules a -nya noun a cause. There are 0 such sentences in `digests/`.
- **#453 (minor, fail-closed).** `_THEN_PADA` refuses "Asap meluas hingga Riau akhir Oktober" and "dari Sabang hingga Merauke akhir Oktober". The #406/#418 behaviour is lost when "pada" is dropped.
- **#454 (minor, fail-closed).** A "diselesaikan pada <time>" is backed only by a source that states the time as an end. "...diselesaikan secara damai pada Oktober 2026" and "diselesaikan Senin (12/10)" do not back it. The IEU-CEPA arc sentence #445 was narrowed for is now refused against its DB source (sitemap 230887, "akhir September selesai"). Whether that arc sentence is fully true is UNVERIFIED: the arc's alur quotes were not traced.
