## Findings (all filed; none is a send-back)
1. **SYS-167 #364 (P3):** a modifier before the table phrase hides it.
   - Symptom: "Senin/Akhir/Awal pekan lalu, Polri memutasi ..." is tied, and not time-led. "Sejak akhir/Senin pekan lalu" counts as an anchor.
   - Root cause: `_RELATIVE` reads "pekan lalu" alone, so the modifier sits in the prefix that `_OPENS_ON_DATE` / `_SEJAK_BEFORE` must match.
   - Scale: 558 articles (26% of the 2,131 "pekan lalu" uses); 0 in the replays.
2. **SYS-168 #365 (P3):** `_FUTURE` misses the future times news writes most. These drop an agenda; their synonym repeats are kept.

   | phrase | articles |
   |---|---|
   | akhir tahun ini | 487 |
   | bulan mendatang | 265 |
   | esok | 191 |
   | akhir bulan ini | 118 |
   | pekan mendatang | 99 |
   | minggu mendatang | 38 |

   For comparison, "minggu depan" (the SYS-166 motivation) is in 348.
3. **SYS-169 #366 (P2):** an agenda's relative future time is never checked against the sources.
   - "Rapat lanjutan ... dijadwalkan besok / minggu depan / pekan ini" is printed when the article says "Kamis (15/10)".
   - The same agenda with an unstated "Jumat (16/10)" is refused.
   - The gap is older (SYS-148); SYS-166 widened it.
4. **SYS-170 #367 (P2):** the SYS-161 guard reads only `<verb> … (men)jadi N`.
   - "bertambah hingga 199" and "bertambah ke angka 199" pass on the same one-event total.
   - Corpus forms:

     | form | articles |
     |---|---|
     | menjadi | 3,462 |
     | hingga/sampai | 914 |
     | ke angka/level | 320 |

   - The replays already hold "naik hingga Rp500 juta".
   - Not filed as their own forms: comma and "menjadi total" (3 and 2 articles).
5. **DBT-79 #368:** 4 QA mutants survive the dev's suites:
   - `qa16_future_latar_not_told` (the dev's own judgement call is untested);
   - `qa16_no_lusa` and `qa16_no_bulan_tahun_depan` (the words moved out of `_SCHEDULED`);
   - `qa16_future_spacing_kept`.
6. **DBT-80 #369:** 27 of 836 registry mutants no longer match their file exactly once.
