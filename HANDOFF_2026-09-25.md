Unit tests passed, the fleet probe passed, and the read-only listing check passed, because the bug is in what gets *stored*, not what gets *listed*. Fixed and verified (179 of 973 now deny, was 0), and 931 rows repaired.

**The generalisation for QA: only MEDIAINDONESIA has executed a real `task=sitemap` since any of this work.** DETIK, OKEZONE, IDXCHANNEL, SINDONEWS and BERITASATU have not. That is where I would expect the next finding.

**2. The gate is drifting down across retrains.** `recall_finance` 0.946 → **0.9405** against a 0.95 floor. Both inside the owner's ±0.02 tolerance, but two consecutive retrains moved the same way; a third would breach. This is a contract decision, not a bug — worth settling before the next retrain rather than after.

**3. The uncertain band nearly doubled** — 0.118 → **0.2045** — because CV re-selected the gate head at `C=1.0` (was 4.0). Nothing in the live decision reads the band, but OPS-18 (#123) and any band-based tie-break would now cover 20% of rows instead of 12%. It is a side effect of a model-selection re-roll, not of the opinion ruling.

**4. `articles.topic` is NULL on 124 rows** — the CNBC/JPNN `opini` articles, denied at the category gate so the probe assigns no topic. **Owner-approved**, and expected to disappear when SYS-48 deletes them. Do not read 11,723/11,847 as drift.

**5. Every remaining occurrence should be read as suspect: it returns its input unchanged on no match, which becomes a URL-shaped key, a URL-shaped category, or a silently collapsed section.

## Two traps worth more than the tickets

**A near-identical pair with opposite labels is a boundary problem, not a model problem.** SYS-49's probe *still* splits the four Iran op-eds when the category is hidden (0.22 keep vs 0.44/0.45/0.55 reject) after the retrain. Moving the boundary to the category fixed it; no amount of tuning would have.

**A title sample tells you what a section is about, not whether there is an article there.** SRC-45 kept `video`/`foto` on 2026-09-22 titles; measuring the extracted body split them — IDXCHANNEL `foto` is 597/842 chars of reporting (kept), its `video` is 205/198 chars of caption (dropped). For any section named after a medium, measure the body.

And the one that stopped a bad fix: the obvious SRC-47 fix — apply the vocabulary to listed URLs — would have dropped **100 hard-news articles per run** from IDXCHANNEL's kept `foto` desk, because its articles live at `/foto-1/foto/…` and `SOFT_BRANCH_EXCEPTIONS` matches whole keys only (`"foto-1" != "foto"`). A first draft of the verification script also reported **512 false leaks** from article slugs before it was restricted to section-like segments. Both are on #157 as acceptance criteria.
