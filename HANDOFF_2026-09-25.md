### Open — deferred by owner decision

**SRC-47 (#157, P3 — deferred 2026-09-25, do not pick this up)** — aggregate feeds bypass the branch-level drop. **1,598 of 22,007 listed URLs (7.3%)** across six sources come from sections already rejected: DETIK 1,185, MEDIAINDONESIA 183 *(closed at the category layer by SRC-48)*, OKEZONE 101, IDXCHANNEL 100, SINDONEWS 27, BERITASATU 2.

**The 7.3% is not the exposure, and this is the number to carry forward.** The filter runs *after* the fetch (`batch_crawling_details` → `classify_batch`), and both a category deny and a probe reject end in the permanent `rejected_filter`, so a leaked URL costs **one fetch either way, fixed or not**. The leaked URLs are soft and the probe already rejects most of them. The real exposure is the probe's ~5% label-noise floor over those 1,598 — on the order of **80 soft articles per cycle** against 11,847 — which is inside the resolution of the labels the probe was accepted on. Owner's test was "≤ 5% is not worth fixing now"; this is inside it.

Deferred with a condition: the standing no-carryover rule places an open `bug` before its code path is **scaled or scheduled**, and this one lives in the listing path that **OPS-19 (#132)** schedules. Re-read it before OPS-19's first unattended run, not before the next phase — a daily crawl across a widened fleet multiplies both the URL count and the frequency, and `OPERATIONS.md`'s per-category health queries (which this ticket degrades on five sources) are how an unattended run is monitored.

An earlier comment of mine on SRC-48 claimed a category deny saves a fetch. It does not; that is corrected on both tickets, and the correction is what re-priced this ticket from P2 to P3.

**SRC-34 (#124)** — groundwork posted: the live leaf inventory for all nine narrowed crawlers. Eight of nine are pinned to a **single leaf** (LIPUTAN6 keeps `bisnis` of 28, TEMPO `ekonomi` of 28, IDNTIMES `business` of 30, INEWS one of 177).

## Two traps worth more than the tickets

**A near-identical pair with opposite labels is a boundary problem, not a model problem.** SYS-49's probe *still* splits the four Iran op-eds when the category is hidden (0.22 keep vs 0.44/0.45/0.55 reject) after the retrain. Moving the boundary to the category fixed it; no amount of tuning would have.

**A title sample tells you what a section is about, not whether there is an article there.** SRC-45 kept `video`/`foto` on 2026-09-22 titles; measuring the extracted body split them — IDXCHANNEL `foto` is 597/842 chars of reporting (kept), its `video` is 205/198 chars of caption (dropped). For any section named after a medium, measure the body.

And the one that stopped a bad fix: the obvious SRC-47 fix — apply the vocabulary to listed URLs — would have dropped **100 hard-news articles per run** from IDXCHANNEL's kept `foto` desk, because its articles live at `/foto-1/foto/…` and `SOFT_BRANCH_EXCEPTIONS` matches whole keys only (`"foto-1" != "foto"`). A first draft of the verification script also reported **512 false leaks** from article slugs before it was restricted to section-like segments. Both are on #157 as acceptance criteria.
