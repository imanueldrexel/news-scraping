## Traps from this audit

- **Mutate with `git checkout` restores, or with `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE=1`.** A same-length mutant restored by file copy kept running from its `.pyc` and faked 7 kills. Tell-tale: one unrelated test "kills" every mutant after a certain point.
- **"`ConnectionError after 5 retries`" can be milliseconds** (SYS-66). Don't read it as "host down".
- **A threshold the sampling design can't reach is dead code** (OPS-18 per-cell). Measure reachable n on production before accepting a gate.
- **Read a sample digest as a reader would.** Counts and unit tests all passed while the digest had 0 real summaries and one story in 5 of 8 slots.
